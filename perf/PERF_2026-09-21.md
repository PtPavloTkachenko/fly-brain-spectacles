# Perf pass, 21.09.2026 — the CLAD method with our own instrument

## What was asked
"Optimise both projects (5.23 and 5.15) through the CLAD performance tools, then apply the knowledge to 5.15."

## What the CLAD tools could and could not do here
- `ls-clad:specs-capture-perf-trace` / `specs-lens-perf-attribution` / `specs-lens-perf-optimize` want a Perfetto
  `.pftrace` from the editor preview (`preview.profiling.startTrace`). On **LS 5.23.2 the session starts, `finish()`
  reports ok, and the file stays 0 bytes** (`[CamPlat.trace] flush() called on stopped session`), whatever the folder or
  the call pattern. No editor trace → `perfetto-trace-analysis` and the optimize loop have no input. (RUNBOOK row.)
- The METHOD is portable and was used: a representative exerciser (page brain + LEAF `start_to_board`), a differential
  sweep with a runnable minimum, one variable per stage, positive deltas only, attribution before any change.
- Instrument: the lens's own probe (`FlySwarm.timed`, board sections) now also lands in the LS log as `PERF_ROW`
  (`fps N worst M ms | perf name=worst/mean …`), summarised by `perf/perf_rows.py`; the sweep is `perf/sweep.py`,
  merged by `perf/sweep_report.py` → `perf/sweep_2026-09-21/SWEEP_RESULTS.md`.
- Device: the page keeps the glasses' 2 s `dbg` row (`LENS_PERF …` in the browser console, `glasses frame` in the
  footer). A real `.pftrace` from the glasses comes from Spectacles Monitor → Start Spectacles Profiling, and THAT is
  what `perfetto-trace-analysis` can read.

## Editor results (Mac preview, LS 5.23.2, page brain, board open, fly shown)
See `sweep_2026-09-21/SWEEP_RESULTS.md` for the table. In one line: the preview frame is ~50 ms (19–20 fps) and the
lens's scripts are ~3.6 ms of it (7 %); no FlyConfig switch moves the frame time beyond noise (±2 ms). Inside the
3.6 ms: the whole update `ours` 1.95 ms (board 0.86, eye rays 0.31, bodies 0.17, vision 0.13, webscene 0.09, fx 0.08),
plus the eye-panel/retina uploads and sound outside it. Switching the eyes to 1 Hz saves 0.75 ms of script; sound 0.3 ms;
scan/Gemini/weather cost nothing at the board (they are async).

## Stages not measured (21.09, honest note)
`03_no_vision`, `07_board_slow`, `08_webscene_slow` never produced a valid window: LEAF's `start_to_board` hung or
was cancelled three runs in a row, Lens Studio 5.23 wedged once mid-LEAF (relaunched; it then rotated its log file
twice, which the runner did not follow), and the page brain was reading a stale log for the PIN. Their rows in
`SWEEP_RESULTS.md` are marked invalid and must not be read as attribution. They are script-side knobs whose editor
effect would be under 1 ms/frame — the conclusion above does not depend on them. Re-run with
`python3 perf/sweep.py 03_no_vision 07_board_slow 08_webscene_slow` on a quiet editor (RUNBOOK: sweep gotchas).

## What this means
- In the editor the frame is render-bound on the Mac (world mesh 64 k verts, the hologram shader, the MSDF board,
  the fly-eye render targets): FlyConfig knobs cannot buy frame time there, and the Mac's renderer is not the glasses'.
- The script side is where the GLASSES differ (a ~5–10x slower CPU): 3.6 ms here is plausibly 20–35 ms there, which
  is the whole budget at 30 fps. The ranked script costs above are the candidate list for the device — the board first,
  then the eye rays/retina, then bodies/vision — but a knob is changed only after a device row says so.
