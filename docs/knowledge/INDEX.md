# CyberFly — Knowledge OS
> **Picking this up cold?** `HANDOFF.md` (21.09) says what is done, what to do next, and the rules that bite.

**Goal (pivot 2026-09-11, ADR 11):** a giant 3D fruit fly in the real room on Spectacles (2024, LS 5.15 project `Spectacles-5.15/`; the LS 5.23 project `Spectacles-5.23/` is the same lens with the LEAF tests), whose every behaviour — walk, turn, take off, fly, feed, groom, sing — is decided by the MaleCNS connectome running on a web page joined by PIN (the Mac server is the editor bench); plus its brain as a live point cloud on the raised hand. Spectacles = senses (world mesh, Gemini, weather, mic, hands) + visualization.

## Where we are (2026-09-21, late)

- **Where a brain runs:** a **web page** joined by PIN (ADR 55, `web/`: WASM + WebGPU, relay), or the Mac (`scripts/run_server.sh`) for the editor bench. `core/` is the one C++ core; **nothing runs natively on the glasses** (ADR 87).
- **Vision, really (15.09, ADR 54):** the 8x16 retina frame was not weak, it was disconnected — 825 viewing directions collapsed into 81 pixels, the photoreceptor saturated 1.03 mV from the top of its range at the calibration light, and R1-R6 is purely inhibitory, so the whole optic lobe sat at 0.00 Hz under every image. The fly now has two ommatidia cameras per eye, the model's own **1,767 columns** (875 left, 892 right) at the real 5 deg spacing, Weber-adapted log contrast, injected as ON/OFF into L1/L2/L5 + Mi1/Tm1/Tm2/Tm9. A loom now fires **LPLC2 22:1** toward the right side through the connectome's own wiring and the escape readout follows. Python == C++ 41/41 identical. Board: the eye mosaic + LC4/LPLC2/LC11/T4/T5 bars.

- **Teaching (ADR 62, 15.09):** the TEACH key runs the real conditioning session from Huang, Luo et al. 2024 — probe, six paired bouts with an unpaired control, probe again — in the room, on the 7,835 KC->MBON synapses (`FlyTrainer.ts`, step-by-step in the guide card, user doc `docs/TEACHING.md`). Verified end to end in the preview; the rule end verified against the core (`core/tools/train_check.py`: PAM11 0.4 -> 92 Hz, efficacy 100 -> 94.3 %, frozen control unmoved). **Open: the socket path could never learn** (`protocol.py` rejected every `learning` frame) — fixed, needs a brain-server restart.
- **Learning, measured (ADR 65, 15.09):** MBON07/MBON11 are on the wire, and a per-thing odour identity (four glomeruli) replaced the one-smell-fits-all `odor`. Three sessions x 4 bouts + a frozen control say the memory forms but is **not cue-specific**: MBON07 falls for the CS+ and the CS- alike, and the two cues fire ~95 % the same Kenyon cells because KC activity is 35-38 % dense at every drive level. The named fix is KC sparsening (APL-style inhibition) — a new mechanism, Pavlo's call.
- **Seven sessions (ADR 66, 15.09):** FOOD, DANGER, FORGET, TEST, CHOICE, TRANSFER, RESET; in the shipped build the on-glasses TEACH key is hidden (`LESSON_ENABLED` false) and a session starts from the page's TRAINING tab (`app.html?tab=training`). Each is one flat plan; the three that only ask a question freeze the synapses while they ask. Instructions per session in `docs/TEACHING.md`; the state also goes to the web page through `FlySceneFeed.train`.
- **The memory IS cue-specific (ADR 67, 15.09, corrects ADR 65):** splitting the plastic synapses by which cue drives them shows -0.010 to -0.020 on the paired cue against its control, three sessions, frozen control exactly 0. The old "not cue-specific" verdict was the global mean and a 4-cell readout hiding a real effect. APL sparsening (`kc_inh_gain`) is built and exact at 1.0, reaches the biological 5-10 % band at 2.0 — and is NOT on, because it makes learning 4x weaker.
- **Teaching is a web-brain feature (ADR 68, 15.09, Pavlo):** the TEACH key stays visible but dim on the glasses alone and says why; with a page connected the page can drive a session itself (`cmd` train / train_stop / reset / memory_set), the lens streams `train`, `things`, `train_summary` and `mem.key` back, and at the end Gemini reads the evidence pack under a prompt that forbids inventing a number. - **She learns by herself, from what happens to her (21.09, ADR 101–103):** pointing at a thing is an INSPECTOR and changes nothing she smells (ADR 101; `present` stays for a thing in the HAND only). The wearer smells — head + hands one glomerulus — and a hand rushing at her or touching her sends a short `punish`, so her smell of the human turns bad (ADR 102, `THREAT_LESSON`). A landing on food or a lure pays `reward` scaled by hunger (0.2 fed … 1.0 hungry — real food never paid before), and heat/cold at the fly is a small pulsed `punish` (ADR 103). `dbg … learn us=r/p last= human= rewardGain=`. **Nothing of this seen on a brain yet.**
- **Learning that lasts (15.09, ADR 63):** the brain's plastic state (the 7,835 KC->MBON synapses) is now a portable blob — `{"memory":"get"|"set"|"clear"|"echo"}` on the core, `FlyMemory.ts` in the lens, the device's own persistent storage as the canonical copy, keyed by the user (so the same person meets the same fly on other glasses) and carrying the fly's name and training history with it. It keeps fading while the glasses are off, with the rule's own tau applied in closed form on load — 1800 s then, 10800 s since ADR 92 (30 min away leaves ~85 %). Proof: `tools/memtest.py` (`echo` 60/60 identical spikes; a trained fly feeds where a naive one flies on), WASM + WebGPU headless, and an end-to-end save/load through the relay in the running lens. **ADR 64** closes the "second learning rule in the central complex" question: the cells and synapses are all there and measured, but the papers' DAN identities cannot be matched and the model has no compass at all, so it stays at one rule.

- **The GPU is exact again, and for a better reason (16.09, ADR 82):** the GPU kernel was failing its own self-check (`gpu_check cells_diff 85673`). It was not the scatter order the way we guessed -- the old kernel's exactness rested on the compiler contracting `evolve` a particular way and on the GPU rebuilding the float scatter's ORDER, and it **fails its own check on the Mac too** past ~10 chunks; we had only ever run three. Now `evolve` uses nothing but correctly-rounded `+ - *` (the two divisions pre-computed into the decay tables, one builder for both kernels) and the synaptic weight is stored as the integer it already is -- `|w|/0.275` is a whole number for 100.000000 % of the 25.1 M edges -- so a tick's deliveries add with `atomicAdd` and no order has to be reproduced. `cells_diff 0` at 3, 10 and 20 chunks on MoltenVK. It deletes the 64 MB record buffer, the sort scratch, the per-target list and both overflow paths (**-79 MB GPU memory**), and costs nothing (CPU +3-5 %, GPU -5 %). FLYB v2 stores `wq` i16 contacts (206.6 -> 156.3 MB; the `.z` only 77.0 -> 72.1). Cost, disclosed: ~1 ULP a tick, which #81's chaos makes a different trajectory -- `{"acc":"fma"}` restores the old arithmetic bit for bit for an A/B. Spot-by-spot: `core/DETERMINISM.md`.

- **Where the hop goes, and the metric that hid it (17.09, ADR 82 cont.):** `gpu_ms` used to mean the LAST 10 ms chunk of the five in a hop, so the GPU's real share had never been on the row. It now means the whole hop (`gpu_chunk_ms` keeps the old meaning), and the core carries a **phase table** — `fb_prof` → `stats()` → the LENS row's `core=` — plus the GPU's own timestamps, so `busy` vs `gpu` separates "the kernel is slow" from "we are queued behind the renderer". On the Mac the phases sum to the step and the GPU is 96-99 %. The page's WASM flags were measured and **not adopted**: `-msimd128` buys **nothing** (+1.0 %, −0.1 %), `-flto` ~2 % for 36 KB, and on WebGPU the whole WASM CPU side is 4 % of a hop. A free proof fell out: the WASM CPU and the native CPU are **bit-identical**.

- **The in-lens brain is gone; a page is the only brain (17.09 ADR 86 switched it off, 20.09 ADR 87 deleted it):** the glasses are senses, motion and a display. Why: the brain was *correct* on the glasses but far too slow there against 50-750 ms on a page, and the path could no longer be verified. **Deleted:** the in-lens brain, `NativeBrainLink.ts`, `BrainLoad.ts`, `TeachGatedNativeScenario.ts`, the build and brain-serving scripts, 31 `NATIVE_` keys. `WebBrainLink` extends `BrainLink` now; the boot card is three stages (SESSION · THE ROOM · THE BRAIN); the trainer logs `reason=no_page`. **Stays, and deleting it would take the web brain with it:** `core/` (the page's WASM/WebGPU brain and the Mac server both compile from it) and its Vulkan kernel (the Mac's GPU path, and the reference the ADR 82 exactness gate checks WebGPU against). With the LAN socket off (ADR 84) and `EXPERIMENTAL_API` gone, the configuration is publishable.
